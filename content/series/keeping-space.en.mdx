This is a series exploring "space". Space can be punctuation, physical space, or time. It can also be the pause between problems and solutions. In a world that always rushes to fill every gap, space gives resilience room to grow and gives us the opportunity to make conscious choices. Because with choices comes freedom, and with freedom, happiness is a byproduct.
